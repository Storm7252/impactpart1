The Method Behind The Metrics

How We Turn
Data Into Growth

Every result you'll see on this page follows the same five-stage engine we've refined across 120+ client engagements. No guesswork. No spray-and-pray. Just a rigorous, repeatable playbook — adapted with surgical precision for each vertical.

"The difference between an agency and a growth partner is accountability. We tie our fees to your outcomes, which means we're as invested in your numbers as you are."

— Founder, Pulse Media

01 /
Discovery & Diagnosis

Before we spend a single rupee of your budget, we spend weeks understanding your business. We audit your existing funnels, analyse competitor positioning, map your audience segments with first-party data, and identify the single biggest lever for growth. Most agencies skip this. We consider it non-negotiable.

Funnel Audit Competitor Analysis Audience Mapping Cohort Research
Clients who complete full discovery see 2.4× better campaign outcomes in the first 90 days vs those who skip to execution.
02 /
Strategy & Architecture

Data becomes direction. We build your full-funnel growth strategy — from awareness channels to retention loops — with clear attribution models, KPI targets tied to business outcomes (not impressions), and a channel mix engineered specifically for your vertical's buying behaviour. Every strategy ships with a 90-day growth roadmap and a defined success scorecard.

Full-Funnel Architecture Attribution Modelling Channel Mix Design KPI Framework
Our attribution model accounts for 7 touchpoints on average before a conversion — most platforms show only the last one.
03 /
Execution & Creative

Strategy without execution is fiction. Our in-house creative, media buying, and SEO teams activate campaigns simultaneously across paid search, paid social, programmatic display, content, email, and WhatsApp — each channel fed with creative that is tested, versioned, and engineered to the platform's native behaviour. We run a minimum of three creative hypotheses per campaign at launch.

Paid Media Creative Testing SEO & Content WhatsApp Automation Email Flows
Our creative lab produces an average of 14 ad variants per client per month, enabling continuous winner identification.
04 /
Measure & Optimise

Every campaign runs inside a live analytics environment we build specifically for your business — custom dashboards that surface what matters to your team, not a generic metrics dump. Weekly review cycles mean underperforming assets are paused within 48 hours and budget is reallocated to winners in real time. We don't wait for monthly reports to make decisions.

Live Dashboards Weekly Reviews A/B Testing Budget Reallocation
On average we identify a winning creative variant within 11 days of launch and double down within 48 hours.
05 /
Scale & Compound

Once a channel-funnel combination proves itself, we scale it deliberately — protecting efficiency metrics while expanding reach. We use lookalike modelling, CRM data enrichment, and cross-channel sequencing to compound results over time. The goal isn't just a great quarter; it's a business that grows more efficiently every month because the data gets smarter.

Lookalike Audiences CRM Enrichment Cross-Channel Sequencing LTV Optimisation
Clients in month 12 average 3.1× better efficiency metrics than they had at month 1 — the compounding effect is real.

Why It Works

Three Beliefs That
Separate Us

Results don't happen by accident. They're the product of a clear philosophy applied consistently — even when clients push for shortcuts. Especially then.

01
Obsess Over Signals, Not Noise

The marketing world is drowning in data but starving for insight. We cut through by focusing only on metrics that have a direct causal relationship with revenue — not the ones that look good in slide decks. Follower counts don't pay salaries. Conversions do.

Every KPI we track for a client must answer one question: "Does improving this number put more money in their pocket?" If it doesn't, we don't track it.

We measure fewer things. Better.
02
Every Vertical Is a Different Language

A healthcare patient doesn't behave like a SaaS buyer. A luxury flat purchase takes 14 weeks; a fashion item takes 14 minutes. We don't recycle strategies across industries. We build from scratch, informed by vertical-specific behavioural data we've accumulated across hundreds of campaigns.

This is why we hire specialists, not generalists. Your healthcare campaigns are run by a team that has only ever worked in healthcare marketing.

Vertical depth beats horizontal breadth.
03
Speed Is a Competitive Advantage

Markets move fast. Consumer sentiment shifts overnight. Algorithms change weekly. The agencies that win are the ones who can identify what's working on Tuesday and scale it by Thursday — not the ones waiting for a monthly review meeting.

Our 48-hour decision cycle, live dashboards, and flat internal structure mean we move faster than the market — and significantly faster than any agency three times our size.

48-hour decision cycles. Always.
